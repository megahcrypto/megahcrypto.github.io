FAQ · Frequently Asked Questions
What is MEGAH Staking?
MEGAH Staking is a program where you lock your MEGAH tokens into a smart contract and earn rewards automatically every second. The more you stake and the longer you stake, the greater the rewards you receive.
How long are my tokens locked after staking?
After staking, your tokens are locked for 90 days. During this period you cannot withdraw the staking principal, but rewards continue to accrue and can be claimed at any time without waiting for the lock to end.
How are my rewards calculated?
Rewards are calculated proportionally based on the amount of MEGAH you have staked relative to the total pool, multiplied by the current APY. The basic formula is:

Reward/sec = (Your Stake ÷ Total Pool) × Reward Rate

A real-time estimate is shown in the "Pending Reward" section of your dashboard.
When can I withdraw my tokens?
Tokens can only be withdrawn after the 90-day lock period ends. The dashboard status will change to "Can Withdraw" and the Withdraw button will become active. Rewards can be claimed at any time without waiting for the lock to end.
What does the "Extend Lock" button do?
The Extend Lock button resets the lock period from now + 90 days forward. This is useful if you want to continue staking longer without having to withdraw and re-stake. Please note that this will reset your lock timer.
Is this smart contract safe?
The smart contract uses the battle-tested OpenZeppelin standard, equipped with ReentrancyGuard (anti-reentrancy attack protection) and SafeERC20 for secure token transfers. The reward token is immutable — it cannot be changed after deployment.
Which wallets are supported?
Supported wallets: MetaMask, Bitget Wallet, WalletConnect, and TokenPocket. The BNB Smart Chain network (Chain ID: 56) will be added automatically when you connect your wallet.
Where can I buy MEGAH tokens?
You can buy MEGAH through: PancakeSwap. Or through other purchase mechanisms such as a public sale, on DEX & etc.
